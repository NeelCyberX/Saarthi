# SAARTHI (सारथी)
### AI-Powered Hyper-Personalized Banking & Digital Lending for Bharat
**Theme:** Digital Transformation in Lending  
**Built for:** Financial Inclusion of Tier 2/3/4 & Rural India (600M+ Citizens)

---

## 🌟 Executive Overview
Indian banks have built extraordinary digital infrastructure (UPI, Net Banking, Video KYC, Account Aggregator), yet **72% of digital loan applications from Tier-2/3/4 towns drop off**. Rural kirana owners, smallholder farmers, and self-help group artisans find conventional banking apps intimidating, generic, and disconnected from their cashflow realities.

**SAARTHI (सारथी)** replaces the one-size-fits-all banking interface with an **empathic, voice-first vernacular guide** that uses sovereign public rails (India Stack) and real-time behavioral AI to deliver contextual credit, audio-guided onboarding, and pre-default stress relief.

---

## 🎯 The Three Core Challenge Pillars

### 1. Proactive Hyper-Personalized Recommendations (NOT Generic Ads)
- **Problem Solved:** Ending spammy credit card pop-ups that irritate rural borrowers.
- **AI Approach:** Analyzes transaction stream telemetry, daily UPI velocities, Mandi procurement slips, and seasonal life-stage signals.
- **Explainability (TreeSHAP):** Every recommendation features a **"🔍 Why this?"** card showing exact attribution factors (e.g. +38% UPI footfall surge, +24% electricity bill regularity).

### 2. Conversational Vernacular Onboarding & Lending ("Saarthi Vani")
- **Problem Solved:** Eliminating high onboarding drop-offs for first-time digital users.
- **Multilingual Voice & Text AI:** Fluent across 7+ Indian languages (**English, हिंदी, ગુજરાતી, मराठी, தமிழ், తెలుగు, বাংলা**).
- **Audio Text-to-Speech (TTS):** Every card and response can be read aloud with native accent synthesis.
- **2-Minute Digital Loan Journey:** Zero paperwork; instant Account Aggregator consent, cashflow underwriting, and Key Fact Statement (KFS) sanction letter generation.

### 3. Early Stress & Fraud Radar with Empathetic Interventions
- **Problem Solved:** Stopping punitive loan default notices and aggressive recovery actions on honest borrowers hit by shocks.
- **AI Stress Autoencoders:** Detects cashflow drops 21 days before formal EMI bounces occur.
- **Compassionate Safeguards:** Automatically freezes high-cost loan cross-sells, waives late fees, protects CIBIL credit score, and activates **30-day grace periods** or **₹80/day micro-bites**.

## 🛡️ Ethical Safeguards & Regulatory Compliance Matrix

| Regulation / Standard | Compliance Principle | SAARTHI Implementation Evidence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DPDP Act 2023** | Lawful Purpose & Explicit Consent | Every Account Aggregator statement fetch generates a cryptographic, time-bound consent artifact. |
| **DPDP Act 2023** | Purpose Limitation | Banking data strictly restricted to underwriting; zero third-party monetization or advertising sharing. |
| **DPDP Act 2023** | Right to Erasure | In-app 1-click consent revocation triggers complete data anonymization upon loan closure. |
| **RBI DLG 2022** | Direct Bank Disbursal | Escrow transfers directly from Regulated Entity to borrower account; no pass-through pooling. |
| **RBI DLG 2022** | Key Fact Statement (KFS) | Pre-execution disclosure of Annual Percentage Rate (APR), repayment terms, and 3-day cooling-off exit. |
| **RBI DLG 2022** | No Silent Limit Hikes | Credit line enhancements strictly require voluntary borrower opt-in. |
| **Algorithmic Fairness** | Mitigation of Demographic Bias | Disparate Impact Ratio > 0.98; women artisans evaluated with equal approval odds to salaried urban peers. |
| **Anti-Predatory Guard** | Debt-Trap Prevention | Stress Index > 0.65 automatically hides all loan upsell banners and triggers debt relief counselors. |
