System never enhances credit limits silently. Every credit increase requires voluntary borrower opt-in.

Hackathon Submission • Theme: Digital Transformation in Lending

SAARTHI (सारथी): AI-Powered Hyper-Personalized Banking & Lending for Bharat

Transforming generic digital banking into a compassionate, vernacular, life-stage financial guide for 600M+ Indians in Tier-2/3/4 towns and rural heartlands.

1. The Problem Statement & Market Opportunity

While India has built world-class sovereign digital rails (UPI processing over 14 billion transactions monthly, DigiLocker, and Account Aggregator), 72% of digital loan applications from Tier-2/3/4 towns drop off. First-time digital borrowers face intimidating English interfaces, rigid monthly lump-sum EMIs, and generic credit card pop-up offers that ignore their cashflow reality. Simultaneously, banks suffer from 40%+ customer acquisition cost (CAC) inflation and rise in SMA-0/1 stress.

2. The SAARTHI AI Solution

SAARTHI replaces the one-size-fits-all digital banking app with a hyper-personalized, voice-first intelligent companion that operates on three breakthrough pillars:

  • Proactive, Life-Stage Product Matching: Instead of generic banner spam, SAARTHI analyzes daily UPI velocity, Mandi procurement receipts, and seasonal cycles to propose working capital lines right when festive inventory or sowing demands it.
  • Conversational Vernacular Onboarding & Lending: Powered by Indic-Whisper and Bhashini LLMs, users interact in their native tongue (Hindi, Gujarati, Tamil, etc.) with real-time speech synthesis and zero paperwork.
  • Empathetic Early Stress Detection: LSTM autoencoders detect cashflow dips 21 days before formal defaults occur, proactively offering 30-day grace periods and micro-daily repayments instead of punitive notices.
